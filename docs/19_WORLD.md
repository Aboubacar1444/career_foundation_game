# 19 — WORLD

## 1. One persistent world
All countries and cities belong to the same game world. A player chooses a country of origin/start, but does not create an isolated regional server.

## 2. Country of origin is identity, not a prison
A player's origin country remains part of their identity even after international success.

Core fields:
- origin country;
- home market;
- current physical location (if location is modeled);
- unlocked markets;
- active markets;
- audience by market;
- career presence by market.

An artist can remain a Malian artist while operating in France, Nigeria, Senegal or another unlocked market.

## 3. Market access
Unlocking a country/market grants access to selected systems from that market without requiring permanent migration.

Examples:
- participate in French battle circuits;
- collaborate with local artists;
- access French media opportunities;
- perform in French venues;
- enter market-specific charts;
- build an audience in France.

## 4. Geographic gating
Activities are attached to markets, cities and eligibility rules. Example: a French battle event may not be available to a new player starting in Mali, while local Mali-specific opportunities are available immediately.

## 5. No forced relocation
International reach is distinct from migration. The game may later model travel or temporary presence for concerts and tours, but international success never requires changing the player's origin country.
