# 30 — WEEKLY EVENTS

## Purpose
Weekly events make the shared world feel alive and provide recurring reasons to play without reducing the game to daily login chores.

## 1. Fixed schedule, adaptive content
Each week has a globally scheduled event theme. The objectives, difficulty, eligible markets, rewards, rivals and narrative context are generated from the player's state and the current world state.

Therefore:

**Calendar = fixed and predictable**

**Content = adaptive to player and world context**

This resolves the distinction between planned weekly events and condition-driven world events: the schedule determines *when* an event family exists; game state determines *what the player actually experiences* inside it.

## 2. Examples
- Battle Week
- Global Collaboration Week
- Festival Week
- Awards Week
- Producer Challenge
- Tour Season
- New Talent Week
- Chart Takeover

## 3. Player scaling
The same world event can produce different objectives by career grade, market access, experience, fanbase and previous participation.

A beginner may receive:
- reach 1,000 streams;
- complete one performance.

A superstar may receive:
- reach a global chart milestone;
- beat a rival in a featured event.

## 4. Shared world, individual progression
An event is global, but the player does not need to be equally powerful to participate. Matchmaking and objective tiers preserve relevance without destroying progression gates.

## 5. Rewards
Rewards may include:
- cash;
- XP;
- speeders;
- cosmetic items;
- temporary boosts;
- reputation opportunities;
- exclusive quest chains;
- certifications or event trophies.

Progression-critical rewards must never depend exclusively on showing up during one specific week.

## 6. Anti-FOMO rule
Missing a weekly event may mean missing part of its immediate competitive window, but it must not create an unrecoverable disadvantage.

The system uses three protections:

1. **Recovery period:** core rewards remain recoverable for a limited period after the event through reduced-scope recovery objectives.
2. **Cyclic return:** major event themes return periodically with new variations, so a missed theme is not permanently lost.
3. **No progression dead-ends:** exclusive cosmetics, titles or short-term bonuses may be time-limited, but essential career progression, unlocks and core economic opportunities must have alternative paths.

The purpose is to create anticipation and competition without making players feel forced to log in every week to protect a career.

## 7. World-event relationship
Weekly events are one scheduled layer of the wider world-event system. A weekly theme establishes the framework; world state and player context determine the actual opportunities, objectives and consequences.
