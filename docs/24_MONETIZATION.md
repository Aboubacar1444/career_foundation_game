# 24 — MONETIZATION

## Principle
Monetization should primarily sell time, convenience, personalization and optional content. It must not make meaningful career success impossible without payment.

## Revenue systems
- production speed-ups;
- tour/operation speed-ups;
- additional convenience slots;
- cosmetics and personalization;
- premium studio customisation;
- premium analytics and planning tools;
- optional narrative/content packs;
- seasonal passes;
- non-essential automation features.

## Guardrails
- no direct bypass of major career unlocks;
- no paid guarantee of chart success;
- no permanent advantage that invalidates progression;
- earned speeders must remain meaningful;
- paid acceleration must save time, not rewrite the rules.
